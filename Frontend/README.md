# Integrated Patient Care Management System – MediTrack

MediTrack is a web-based Integrated Patient Care Management System designed to make healthcare management simple, organized, and efficient.

The system provides a centralized platform for managing patient information, appointments, consultations, prescriptions, notifications, and healthcare activities. It helps patients, doctors, and administrators manage their respective tasks through a structured digital system.

## Key Features

- Patient Registration and Profile Management
- Appointment Scheduling and Slot Management
- Doctor Consultation and Diagnosis Management
- Prescription and Treatment History
- Patient Medical Records
- Notifications and Appointment Reminders
- Role-Based Access for Patients, Doctors, and Administrators
- Authentication and Security
- REST API Integration
- Audit Logging and Security Monitoring
- Healthcare Analytics and Reports

## Project Objective

The main objective of MediTrack is to reduce manual paperwork, improve access to patient information, simplify appointment management, and provide a secure and organized healthcare workflow.

## Project Structure

The project is developed in multiple milestones:

1. **Milestone 1 – Manage:** Patient registration, profiles, database, and appointments.
2. **Milestone 2 – Treat:** Consultation, diagnosis, treatment history, and prescriptions.
3. **Milestone 3 – Protect & Connect:** Security, authentication, APIs, notifications, and audit logging.
4. **Milestone 4 – Analyze & Finalize:** Analytics, reports, testing, optimization, documentation, and deployment.

## Future Scope

The system can be further enhanced with online payments, laboratory integration, pharmacy management, video consultations, SMS/email notifications, and advanced healthcare analytics.

## Conclusion

MediTrack aims to provide a secure, user-friendly, and efficient digital solution for managing patient care and healthcare operations.
